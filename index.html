The idea

Own your future

AI is changing work and life fast, and a lot of people are scared. I'd rather get ready than panic. My answer is to own things.

  • Own your AI

    Models that run on hardware you own, with your data staying yours.

  • Own your work

    Tools, code and data you can open, fix and keep.

  • Own your body

    Strength and health you build yourself. No app does the reps for you.

  • Own your mind

    Skills that stay with you when the tools change.

Hire the blacksmith.
Own the sword.

I use cloud AI every day. That's hiring the blacksmith, and it's fine. What matters is that you own what comes out of it: your tools, your code, your data, your skills, your health.

A borrowed sword can be taken back.

So I test what runs on my PC against the cloud, honestly. When the cloud wins, I say so. Own the floor, rent the peak.
